Our Mission & Vision

For over two decades, the Odisha Society of Oncology has been at the forefront of cancer care in Eastern India. Our mission is to reduce the burden of cancer through awareness, prevention, early detection, and compassionate support for patients and their families.

We believe that every individual deserves access to quality cancer care regardless of their socio-economic status. Through our various programs and initiatives, we aim to create a cancer-free Odisha.

Our Services

We provide comprehensive cancer care services to support patients at every stage of their journey.

Cancer Screening

Early detection through regular screening programs for various types of cancers.

Treatment Guidance

Expert guidance on treatment options, hospitals, and specialists.

Patient Counseling

Psychological support for patients and families dealing with cancer diagnosis.

Radiation & Chemo Support

Assistance and care coordination for radiation and chemotherapy treatments.

Tobacco Awareness

Community programs to raise awareness about tobacco-related cancer risks.

Financial Help

Financial assistance for underprivileged patients for treatment and medication.

Awareness Programs

Our community outreach initiatives to educate and empower people about cancer prevention.

Breast Cancer Awareness

Monthly workshops and screening camps for early detection of breast cancer.

Tobacco-Free Odisha

Campaigns in schools and workplaces to reduce tobacco consumption.

Free Screening Camps

Regular free screening camps in rural and underserved areas.

Survivor Meetups

Monthly support group meetings for cancer survivors to share experiences.
